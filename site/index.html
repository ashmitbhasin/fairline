    Fair value

    Pinnacle takes the sharpest bets there are and keeps its line, so its price is the reference. Fairline removes Pinnacle's margin, compares the result with Kalshi's price, and charges Kalshi's fee before calling anything an edge.

    The calculator runs the same formulas as the repo. Change the line.

    Record

    Two rules, replayed minute by minute over the tape and scored when Kalshi settled each market. Take buys when fair beats the price by a threshold after the taker fee. Quote rests a bid and an offer either side of fair, and fills only when a trade prints through it.

    The first replay showed the 2¢ take rule winning. Both of its bets were artifacts: one priced off a recorded book that was wrong, the other off a Pinnacle price that had sat unchanged for ten minutes while other books moved on news. With guards for both, the record reads like this.
